Applies the rules above to trades you switch to automanage: on the simulated account at any level, on the Schwab account up to Propose. Notify sends a message; Propose prepares an order for one tap; Auto places it at mid and steps toward natural. These levels apply to every trade with Automanage ticked on Positions.

Management rules: opening trades

The scanner looks once a day, at the scan time, for premium worth selling: a strangle when IV rank is high, an iron condor otherwise, in the monthly expiration nearest the target DTE. What it finds follows the level below: Notify, Propose (waits for your tap) or Auto (simulated account only; live stops at Propose). Single stocks open only when their next earnings report is known and further away than the buffer; ETFs and indexes have no earnings.
