Portfolio targets

tastytrade mechanics: trade small, trade often, and manage the portfolio as a whole. Delta is beta-weighted to SPY and shown as a share of net liq (SPY deltas times SPY's price, over net liq): +10% means the account gains about 1% of net liq on a 10% SPY rally. A short strangle opens near delta neutral and turns short delta as the market rises and long delta as it falls; tastytrade keeps the book near neutral and leans it only by choice. Daily theta of 0.1% to 0.3% of net liq is tastytrade's published guide: less leaves buying power idle, more usually means too much risk for the account.

Outside a band the app corrects by what it opens, never by a hedge trade of its own: the header turns amber, a notification goes out once a day, the order ticket warns about a trade that moves delta or theta further out, and the scanner leans new strangles and iron condors back toward the delta band (puts closer when too short, calls closer when too long), proposes rather than places a trade that would push delta further out, and opens nothing while theta is at its maximum. The bias bands are this app's defaults, not tastytrade figures; widen them for a small account, where one trade moves delta a lot.

Management rules: automanage

Applies the rules above to trades you switch to automanage: on the simulated account at any level, on the Schwab account up to Propose (Auto too once Allow Auto is on under Live trading). Notify sends a message; Propose prepares an order for one tap; Auto places it at mid and steps toward natural. These levels apply to every trade with Automanage ticked on Positions.

Rolling the untested side closer collects more credit and brings the trade back toward neutral; matching the tested side's delta re-centres it.

tastytrade mechanics: the guts can never be worth less than their in-the-money value (an inverted strangle, at least its inversion width), so the repair pays up to that plus your allowance and locks in no new loss beyond it; the new 16-delta strangle keeps collecting theta with the breakevens re-centred. A straddle has no untested side to roll closer, so until a short is this deep it is left to the profit target (25% for straddles) and the 21 DTE rule. tastytrade sets no fixed delta for this repair; 0.70 is this app's default, a short strike roughly half a standard deviation in the money.

Management rules: opening trades

The scanner looks once a day, at the scan time, for premium worth selling: a strangle when IV rank is high, an iron condor otherwise, in the monthly expiration nearest the target DTE. What it finds follows the level below: Notify, Propose (waits for your tap) or Auto (the Schwab account stops at Propose unless Allow Auto is on under Live trading). Single stocks open only when their next earnings report is known and further away than the buffer; ETFs and indexes have no earnings.
