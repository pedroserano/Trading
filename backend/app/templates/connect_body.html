Core shares

Stock you hold apart from options trading, one line each: AAPL 200. Up to that many shares, and the covered calls written on them (one per 100), are left out of the portfolio's delta: the header's figure, the delta band, the scanner's lean and automanage's portfolio view. Shares above the number still count, as does every other option on the stock. Net liq, buying power and P/L are unchanged.

A key lets an AI assistant (through MCP) or a script (through the /api endpoints) use the app. Each key has a level. A key never changes settings, views, keys or the Schwab sign-in, and live orders still pass the live list, the cash and sweep check (never margin), Schwab's preview and the kill switch. You get a notification for every order a key places. Read the AI guide: what an AI can do with the app, and how to connect Claude over Tailscale.
